Condo & Apartment Movers in Toronto

Jasper Moving helps with condo, apartment and high-rise moves throughout Toronto and the GTA — with the extra planning that building access, elevators and loading docks require.

Get a Free Condo Moving Quote

Moving into or out of a condo or apartment isn't quite the same as moving out of a house. High-rise buildings add extra steps: booking an elevator, coordinating a loading dock or underground parking, checking in with concierge or security, and getting furniture through narrower hallways and doorways without a scratch. Add a building's own move-in or move-out schedule on top of that, and a condo move takes more coordination than most people expect. Jasper Moving plans around all of it, so your move-day timeline lines up with what your building actually allows.

Planning Ahead

Toronto Condo Moves Require Careful Planning

A smooth condo move usually comes down to a few practical details, sorted out before moving day.

Elevator Reservations

Many Toronto condos require residents to reserve a service elevator for moving. Booking windows are often limited, so we recommend confirming this with your building as soon as you have a move date.

Building Management Requirements

Some buildings have specific moving hours, access procedures, or documentation requirements — such as a certificate of insurance — before a move can go ahead. We're happy to work with whatever your building needs.

Loading Dock & Parking Access

Where a building has a loading dock or designated moving area, coordinating truck access ahead of time helps the whole move run on schedule instead of losing time at the curb.

Downtown Toronto Access

Downtown moves come with their own practical challenges — traffic, limited loading space, and building access that can vary block by block. We factor this into how we plan the day.

Furniture & Property Protection

Elevators, hallways, doorways and floors in shared spaces get protected along with your own furniture, so both your belongings and the building are handled with care.

Elevator availability, loading dock access and building permissions are set by your property management, not by us — we plan carefully around them, but we can't guarantee what a building will approve or how quickly they'll respond. Getting boxes packed and ready before your moving window helps too — see our packing services in Toronto & the GTA page for more.

Get Ready

Before Your Condo Moving Day

A few things worth sorting out ahead of time, whatever moving company you use.

  • Contact your property management to let them know about your move date
  • Confirm whether your building requires an elevator reservation
  • Confirm your building's allowed moving hours
  • Ask about loading dock or designated moving area access
  • Check any parking or loading restrictions near your building
  • Measure large furniture and doorways to plan for tight turns
  • Reserve elevators or loading docks as early as your building allows
  • Inform concierge or security if your building requires advance notice
  • Prepare keys, fobs or access cards for both your old and new unit

FAQ

Condo & Apartment Moving Questions

Do I need to reserve an elevator for a condo move in Toronto?

Many Toronto condo buildings require residents to book the service elevator in advance for moving day. Requirements vary by building, so it's best to confirm with your property management as early as possible.

How early should I book condo movers?

We recommend booking as soon as you have a move date, especially if your building requires an elevator reservation or has limited moving time slots. This gives you time to coordinate both your move and your building's requirements.

Can movers help with apartment moves without an elevator?

Yes. We move apartments in walk-up buildings and buildings without elevators. Let us know about stairs or building layout in advance so we can plan crew time and equipment accordingly.

What should I ask my condo management before moving?

Ask about elevator booking procedures, allowed moving hours, loading dock or parking access, any required insurance certificates, and whether concierge or security needs advance notice.

Can you move furniture from high-rise condos?

Yes, we regularly move furniture in and out of high-rise condos, including larger items that need careful handling through elevators, hallways and doorways.
