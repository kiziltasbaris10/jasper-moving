Moving in the City

What a Toronto Residential Move Looks Like

Moving within Toronto often comes with logistics you won't find in a smaller town. Here's how we plan around them.

  • Condo & apartment elevator bookings: Many downtown Toronto buildings require a booked freight elevator and a set move-in window. We coordinate timing with your building management so your move stays on schedule.
  • Loading areas & building access: From underground loading docks to street-level entrances, we plan the route in and out of your building ahead of time to avoid delays on moving day.
  • Downtown parking & access challenges: Narrow streets, permit parking and limited truck access are common in Toronto's core. We factor this into move-day planning so the truck can load and unload efficiently.
  • Protecting floors, walls & doorways: Hallways, elevators and stairwells get protected at both your old and new address to help avoid scuffs and damage in shared spaces.
  • Move-day coordination: We confirm timing, access details and any building requirements with you in advance, so there are no surprises when the crew arrives.

FAQ

Residential Moving Questions

Do you move apartments and condos in downtown Toronto?

Yes. We regularly move apartments and condos across downtown Toronto and coordinate with building management on elevator bookings and access requirements.

Do I need to book a moving elevator myself?

Some buildings require the resident to book the elevator directly with management. We'll confirm what your building requires and plan our arrival time around it.

Will you protect my floors and walls during the move?

Yes, we use floor runners, corner guards and door protection at both your old and new address to help protect shared and private spaces.

Do I need to be home on moving day?

We recommend having someone available to answer questions and confirm placement of items, but we'll work with you to find an arrangement that fits your schedule.
