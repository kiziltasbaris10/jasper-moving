Jasper Moving helps customers prepare and pack belongings for residential moves in Toronto and the GTA — packing support can make move preparation more organized and a lot more manageable.

Get a Free Packing Quote

Packing is one of the most time-consuming parts of a move, and it's easy to underestimate how much there is to do. Beyond simply putting things in boxes, a well-organized pack means belongings are grouped sensibly, furniture and household items are protected before they're loaded, boxes are labelled clearly enough to be useful on the other end, and fragile items are prepared so they survive the trip. It also means thinking ahead about what actually needs to be packed weeks in advance versus what should stay out until the last day — and, for condo and apartment movers, working around hallways, elevators and building move-out schedules. Done well, packing turns moving day from a scramble into something that's already mostly planned.

Getting Started

What Should You Pack First When Moving?

A sensible packing order keeps daily life running while you prepare for the move.

1

Seasonal and rarely used belongings

Off-season clothing, holiday decorations and storage items you won't need before moving day.

2

Decorations and non-essential items

Artwork, ornaments and anything decorative that doesn't affect day-to-day living.

3

Books and miscellaneous household items

These add up quickly and are easy to pack well before moving day.

4

Extra clothing and linens

Keep a few days' worth out and pack the rest as your move date approaches.

5

Kitchen items not needed daily

Small appliances, extra dishes and specialty cookware you're not using this week.

6

Everyday belongings closer to moving day

Keep the essentials you use daily accessible until the last possible moment.

Careful Handling

Preparing Fragile Items for a Move

  • Wrap fragile items individually with appropriate protective materials before boxing them
  • Avoid leaving excessive empty space inside boxes, which allows items to shift in transit
  • Label fragile boxes clearly on multiple sides so they're handled carefully at every stage
  • Avoid overloading any box — heavier items should generally go in smaller boxes
  • Keep components of the same item together, such as hardware for furniture or matching sets
  • Separate important personal valuables and keep them with you rather than in with general packing
Room by Room

Packing Your Home Room by Room

Kitchen

Separate everyday essentials from items that can be packed earlier, and wrap dishes and glassware individually.

Bedroom

Prepare clothing, linens and personal belongings systematically, working from off-season items toward daily-use pieces.

Living Room

Pack decor, electronics and media early, keeping remotes and cables labelled and together.

Bathroom

Pack extra toiletries and supplies ahead of time, keeping a small bag of daily essentials out until moving day.

Home Office

Organize cables, label electronics, and keep important documents accessible rather than boxed away early.

Storage Areas

Basements, closets and garages often hold items that haven't been touched in a while — a good place to start packing early.

Get Ready

Before Your Packing Day

A few things worth sorting out before boxes start filling up.

  • Declutter belongings and decide what's actually moving with you
  • Separate items you'll personally transport rather than pack with the rest
  • Identify important documents and keep them accessible
  • Set aside medications so they're not packed away by mistake
  • Responsibly dispose of items that shouldn't be transported
  • Organize valuables separately from general packing
  • Identify fragile belongings ahead of time so they get extra care
  • Confirm building access at your current address if applicable
  • Label destination rooms so boxes end up in the right place
  • Prepare an essentials bag for the first day or two in your new place
  • Discuss any unusual or oversized items with your mover before moving day

If you're not sure whether an item can be transported, confirm with your mover ahead of time rather than assuming.

City Living

Packing for Toronto Condo & Apartment Moves

Packing for a condo or apartment comes with a few extra considerations beyond a house move. Limited space means it helps to pack in stages rather than filling every room with boxes at once, and keeping hallways and common areas clear matters in a shared building. Elevator timing is worth planning around too — having your boxes organized and ready before your building's moving window opens keeps things moving efficiently once the elevator is booked. Labelling clearly and preparing furniture in advance for disassembly also go a long way. For more on managing building access and elevator bookings specifically, see our condo and apartment moving guide.

FAQ

Packing Services Questions

When should I start packing for a move?

It's generally a good idea to start with seasonal and rarely used items several weeks before your move, then work toward everyday belongings as moving day gets closer. Starting early reduces last-minute pressure.

Can I get help packing only certain rooms?

Yes, packing support can be arranged for selected rooms or belongings rather than the whole home. Let us know what you'd like help with when you request your quote.

How should fragile items be packed for moving?

Fragile items generally do best with appropriate wrapping, minimal empty space inside the box, and clear labelling so they're handled carefully. Avoid overloading any single box, fragile or not.

What should I keep with me instead of packing on the moving truck?

Important documents, medications, valuables and anything you'd need immediate access to are best kept with you rather than packed onto the truck.

How should I prepare for packing a condo or apartment?

Packing tends to go more smoothly in a condo or apartment when boxes are organized and ready before your building's moving window, and hallways and elevators stay clear during loading.

Are packing materials included with packing services?

What's included can vary depending on the move. Please confirm required materials and what's included when you request your quote.
