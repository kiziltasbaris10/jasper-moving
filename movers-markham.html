Markham has grown into one of the GTA's larger municipalities largely through master-planned residential development, and that shows in how the city is laid out. Communities built out over the last couple of decades — places with a consistent mix of detached homes, semi-detached houses and townhomes — sit alongside older, more established neighbourhoods, while areas like Markham Centre have added newer condo towers and mixed-use buildings closer to office and commercial space. Because so much of Markham was built with planning in mind, streets and building access tend to follow fairly predictable patterns, but the property type still shapes what a move actually looks like: a townhome move is about driveways and staged loading, a condo move comes down to elevator bookings and building windows, and an office move has its own access and equipment considerations. Jasper Moving plans around whichever of these applies to your move.

Local Planning

Planning a Move in Markham

A few practical details tend to matter most, depending on the property types involved.

House & Townhome Moves

On residential streets, planning tends to focus on driveway and garage access, stairs and entrances, and preparing larger household items and furniture in a sensible loading order.

Condo & Apartment Moves

Where a move involves a condo or apartment building, it's worth checking whether a service elevator reservation is required, what the building's moving window looks like, how underground parking and loading areas work, and what property management needs to know in advance.

Traffic & Route Planning

Markham connects to several major routes, including Highway 404, Highway 407 and Highway 7, which can factor into how a route between addresses gets planned. We take typical conditions into account, though we can't control traffic or promise a specific travel time on the day.

Parking & Loading Access

Checking parking and loading access at both your current and new address ahead of time helps the move stay on schedule once the truck arrives.

High-Density Living

Condo & Apartment Moving Around Markham Centre

Markham Centre has developed into a denser, mixed-use area with a growing number of condo towers alongside office and retail space. Moving in or out of one of these buildings usually comes down to a handful of practical details: whether the building requires a service elevator booking, what the assigned moving window looks like, how underground parking and loading access work, and what concierge or property management needs to know ahead of time. Access fobs and measuring furniture against elevator and hallway dimensions are also worth sorting out before moving day. We don't have any special arrangements or relationships with individual condo management offices — elevator bookings and access approvals are entirely up to your building — but confirming these details early and letting us know means the move can be planned properly around them. See our condo and apartment moving services for more on how we generally handle these moves.

Residential Moves

House & Townhome Moving in Markham

Markham's residential neighbourhoods — from established areas like Unionville to newer townhome communities elsewhere in the city — are made up mostly of detached and semi-detached houses and townhome complexes, and these moves come with their own practical considerations. Driveway access and entrance width are worth checking in advance, along with stairs that larger furniture will need to pass through. Protecting floors and doorways at both ends helps avoid unnecessary wear, and labelling boxes by destination room as you pack makes unloading and placing them at the new address far more straightforward. Preparing the destination ahead of time — knowing where larger pieces should go and confirming access — matters just as much as getting ready to leave. See our residential moving services for more on how we approach full house moves.

Business Moves

Commercial & Office Moving in Markham

Markham has a substantial amount of office and commercial space, and business relocations come with different priorities than a residential move. Organizing office furniture and equipment ahead of time, clearly labelling what goes where, and planning loading and access at both the origin and destination all help keep the process organized. Coordinating the move around business operations matters too, though we can't guarantee a specific window of downtime — that depends on the scope of the move and factors outside our control. We don't have partnerships with specific Markham businesses or specialized commercial certifications to point to; we simply plan carefully around whatever your business relocation involves. See our commercial moving services for more detail.

Get Ready

Before Moving Day in Markham

A few practical reminders, whatever moving company you use.

  • Confirm condo or building rules where applicable, such as move-in or move-out hours
  • Reserve an elevator or loading area in advance if your building requires one
  • Check driveway or loading access at both addresses
  • Verify parking restrictions at both your current and new address
  • Prepare keys, fobs or access cards for both addresses
  • Measure large furniture and doorways to plan for tight turns
  • Identify furniture that may need to be disassembled ahead of time
  • Label boxes by destination room so unpacking is easier
  • Identify fragile items ahead of time so they get extra care
  • Confirm access details at your destination address in advance
  • Keep essential documents accessible rather than packed away

FAQ

Markham Moving Questions

How far in advance should I book movers in Markham?

We recommend booking as soon as you have a move date, especially if your building requires an elevator reservation or your move involves larger furniture that needs extra preparation. This gives more time to plan around any building or access requirements.

Do Markham condo buildings require elevator reservations?

Some condo and apartment buildings in Markham require an elevator booking for moving day, while others don't. Requirements vary by building, so it's best to confirm directly with your property management.

Can you help with house and townhome moves in Markham?

Yes, we help with house and townhome moves throughout Markham, including furniture preparation and planning around driveway or garage access.

What should I confirm before moving into a Markham condo?

Ask about elevator booking procedures, allowed moving hours, loading area or underground parking access, any required insurance certificates, and whether concierge or security needs advance notice.

How should I prepare driveway, parking or loading access for moving day?

Check for visitor parking restrictions, driveway space, or loading zone rules at both your current and new address, and let us know about anything unusual so we can plan around it.
