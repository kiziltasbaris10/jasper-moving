Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Jasper Moving ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It's written to be plain and short — if anything is unclear, contact us at info@jaspermoving.ca.

Information we collect

When you submit a quote request or contact form on this site, we collect the information you provide directly: name, phone number, email address, moving details (origin/destination, move date, move type), and any message you include. We do not collect this information through any other means — there are no tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or advertising cookies on this site at this time.

How we use it

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your quote request or inquiry
  • Plan and coordinate a move, if you become a customer
  • Follow up about your request by phone or email

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party form processing

Quote and contact form submissions on this site are processed by Formspree, a third-party form backend service, which delivers submissions to our email inbox. Formspree's own privacy practices apply to the transmission of that data; see Formspree's Privacy Policy for details.

Data retention

We retain quote request information for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a customer, for the duration of our business relationship plus a reasonable period for record-keeping. You can request deletion of your information at any time (see below).

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, correct inaccuracies, and request that it be deleted, subject to legal or legitimate business record-keeping requirements. To exercise any of these rights, email info@jaspermoving.ca.

Cookies

This website does not currently use cookies for tracking or advertising. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach us at info@jaspermoving.ca or (647) 555-0123.
