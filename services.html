FAQ

Common questions about our services

How much does it cost to hire movers in the GTA?

Local moves are typically priced hourly based on crew size; long-distance moves are priced by weight and distance. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your move.

Is Jasper Moving licensed and insured?

Yes — fully licensed and insured, with basic liability coverage included and full-value protection available as an add-on.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes, including boxes, wrap, and specialty crating, plus full or partial packing service if you'd rather we handle it.

Do you handle moves from Canada to the United States?

Yes — we offer cross-border relocation services from the GTA to destinations across the U.S., including support with customs documentation. Get in touch for a free cross-border quote.
