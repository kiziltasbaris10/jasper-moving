Get Ready

Before Your Long-Distance Moving Day

Practical steps that make a longer move easier, whichever moving company you choose.

  • Confirm your move-out and move-in dates at both locations
  • Prepare an inventory of what's being moved
  • Decide what you're not taking with you
  • Measure large furniture against doorways and stairwells
  • Confirm building access at both your old and new address
  • Reserve elevators or loading docks where required
  • Prepare keys, fobs or access cards for your new place
  • Keep essential documents with you rather than on the truck
  • Pack medications and valuables separately
  • Label boxes by room to make unpacking easier
  • Prepare an essentials box for your first night in the new place
  • Confirm parking and access details at your destination
What's Different

Local Moving vs. Long-Distance Moving

A local move and a long-distance move both involve the same basic work — packing, loading, transporting and unloading — but a longer-distance move generally needs more advance coordination simply because the origin and destination are farther apart. Scheduling has less room for last-minute changes, preparation and inventory planning matter more since there's less flexibility to run back for something forgotten, and access at both ends needs to be worked out ahead of time rather than sorted out on the day. The core difference isn't the work itself so much as how far ahead it needs to be planned.

FAQ

Long-Distance Moving Questions

How far in advance should I book a long-distance move?

The earlier the better. Long-distance moves involve more coordination than local moves, so booking as soon as you have a move date gives more flexibility for scheduling and planning.

How should I prepare furniture for a long-distance move?

Larger furniture is often disassembled where practical, and items are wrapped and protected for the longer time they'll spend loaded and in transit. Let us know about any pieces that need special handling ahead of time.

What should I keep with me instead of putting on the moving truck?

Essential documents, medications, valuables and anything you'd need immediate access to are best kept with you rather than packed onto the truck for a long-distance move.

How should I prepare for moving into a condo or apartment in another city?

Confirm elevator or loading dock requirements, parking restrictions and any building access rules with your new property management ahead of time, the same way you would for a local condo or apartment move.

How is a long-distance move different from a local move?

A long-distance move generally needs more advance coordination since the origin and destination are farther apart, with more planning around inventory, scheduling and access at both ends.
