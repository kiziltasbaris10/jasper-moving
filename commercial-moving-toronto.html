A commercial move isn't just a bigger version of a home move. It usually means coordinating around business hours, moving desks and workstations without losing track of where everything belongs, handling shared equipment carefully, and working within a building's elevator and loading area rules — sometimes across more than one location at once. Good planning can't promise zero disruption to your business, but it goes a long way toward keeping the move organized and predictable. Jasper Moving works with you ahead of time on scheduling, access and logistics, so moving day is planned rather than improvised.

Planning Ahead

Planning a Commercial Move in Toronto

A handful of practical decisions, made early, tend to make the biggest difference on move day.

Business Continuity

Scheduling the move around your slower periods and preparing staff in advance helps reduce disruption, even though no move can guarantee zero impact on operations.

Building Access

Elevators, loading docks and property management requirements vary by building. We work with whatever access procedures your current and new location require.

Office Furniture & Workstations

Desks, chairs, shelving and workstations are moved with the same care as any other furniture, with disassembly and reassembly where it's needed.

Equipment Handling

Shared office and business equipment is handled carefully during loading, transport and unloading, with attention to how it's packed and positioned in the truck.

Labelling & Organization

Clear labelling and a destination plan for each area or department make unloading faster and reduce the chance of items ending up in the wrong spot.

After-Hours & Weekend Planning

Many businesses prefer to move outside regular operating hours. We offer after-hours and weekend scheduling so the move can happen around your business, not the other way around.

Get Ready

Before Your Office Moving Day

Practical steps worth taking ahead of time, whichever moving company you work with.

  • Notify employees of the move date and any changes to their workspace
  • Coordinate move timing and access with property management at both locations
  • Confirm elevator or loading dock booking requirements
  • Label workstations and furniture by destination area or department
  • Create a destination floor plan for the new space
  • Identify priority equipment that needs to be set up first
  • Prepare access cards, fobs or keys for both locations
  • Confirm parking and loading restrictions near both buildings
  • Back up important business data before moving IT equipment

FAQ

Commercial Moving Questions

How far in advance should an office move be planned?

We recommend starting to plan several weeks ahead, especially if your building requires elevator or loading dock bookings, or if the move involves IT equipment and multiple departments. Earlier planning gives more flexibility around scheduling.

Can an office move be scheduled outside normal business hours?

Yes, we offer after-hours and weekend scheduling for office and commercial moves, so you can plan around your business hours rather than around ours.

What should we do with computers and IT equipment before moving?

Back up important business data before moving day, and let us know which equipment needs careful, server-safe handling so we can plan accordingly. Disconnecting and labelling cables in advance also helps speed up setup at the new location.

Do commercial buildings require elevator or loading dock reservations?

Many office buildings require booking a freight elevator or loading dock in advance, particularly downtown. We recommend confirming this with your building management as early as possible.

How should office furniture and workstations be prepared?

Clearing personal items, labelling desks and workstations by destination, and identifying any furniture that needs disassembly all help the move go more smoothly and make unloading easier to organize.
